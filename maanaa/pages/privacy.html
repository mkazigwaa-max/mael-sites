Privacy

What we collect, and why.

The short version: we collect what an order and an assessment need, we use it to run your order and your sequence, and we don't sell it. The long version follows — and where the two ever disagree, the short version is the promise.

Assessment answers

Your seven answers classify your constraint and generate your Constraint Card. They're stored with your email only if you give it, and they're used to recommend one product and run the Day 0/2/5 sequence — not to build an advertising profile for sale.

Orders + subscriptions

Name, address, payment details (processed by our payment provider; we don't store full card numbers), order history, and subscription state — what's needed to ship, charge exactly what your Manage page shows, and honor the guarantee.

What we never do

We don't sell personal data. We don't enroll you in marketing from a purchase without consent. We don't keep a cancelled customer's data working against them — cancel stops charges and marketing alike.

Your controls

Unsubscribe from any email in one click. Request your data or its deletion through support; deletion removes everything except the order records the law requires us to retain.

This policy takes effect at launch on getmaanaa.com and will be versioned here with dates if it ever changes.
