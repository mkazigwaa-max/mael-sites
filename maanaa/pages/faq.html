FAQ

Asked, answered — straight.

How does the assessment work?

Seven questions, about 45 seconds. It classifies your focus constraint — Fuel, Anchor, Guard, or Catalyst — and recommends exactly one product matched to it. You see your result with no email required; the email is only for sending your Constraint Card and the follow-up sequence.

Am I subscribed if I buy once?

No. One-time purchases are one-time. A subscription starts only if you explicitly choose it at checkout, and your Manage page shows the next charge date and amount from day one.

How do I cancel?

Two taps on your Manage page. No chat, no phone call, no retention maze. Cancelled subscriptions never charge again and never restart themselves.

What does the guarantee cover?

Everything, for 90 days from delivery: full refund of product and shipping, with return shipping on us via a prepaid label. The full text lives on the Guarantee page — and it's printed inside every box.

Why do some products list fewer actives than competitors?

Because formats have physics. A melt strip carries ~150 mg total; we print what fits at real amounts instead of listing twelve ingredients at dust levels. Every active and its job is on the Doses page.

How fresh is what ships?

Nothing leaves with less than 20% of its shelf life remaining, picked first-expiry-first-out. BEST BY and PACKED ON are printed on every pack, and each lot's test results live in the COA library.

When do the other products launch?

In waves, in the order the economics and the formats earn: Wave 2 brings SIGNAL and STEADY, Wave 3 ROOT and CHARGE, Wave 4 RISE. The assessment waitlist is the first to know.

What if I feel nothing?

Some people don't — every Timeline Card says so in print, before you buy. That's precisely what the 90-day guarantee is for. Use it; that's what it's there for.
