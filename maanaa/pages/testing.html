The COA library

Trust, filed by lot number.

Every MAANAA pack prints its lot number and a QR code. Scan it, and you land here — on that exact lot's certificate of analysis. No lot ships without a COA and retained samples. That's not a marketing line; it's the shipping rule, and this library is where it gets audited.

What every lot must pass

The release checklist, published.

IDENTITY + POTENCY

What's on the label is in the pack

Every active assayed against its printed amount. If a lot assays low, it doesn't ship — it isn't "blended off" against a stronger lot.

PURITY

Heavy metals + microbials

Standard contaminant panels per lot, results on the COA — the numbers, not a "tested" badge.

FORMAT SPECS

Each format has its own gate

SIGNAL tabs must dissolve clear — no strands, no floaters. ROOT and CHARGE (oil-bearing) carry a peroxide-value oxidation limit. SPARK films are assayed against the printed 150 mg-format table.

RETAINED SAMPLES

Kept, not just claimed

Samples from every lot are retained past its shelf life. If a customer ever questions a pack, the lot itself answers.
