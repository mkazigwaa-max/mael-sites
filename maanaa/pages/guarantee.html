The Anti-Gundry page

Every rule they broke,
we publish.

Before building MAANAA, we read thousands of one-star reviews of the biggest supplement brands in America. Almost none of the anger was about the products. It was about the money: subscriptions people didn't know they'd joined, refunds that needed a lawyer's patience, "guarantees" that cost money to use. So the money rules got built first. Here they are, in plain words, before you spend a dollar.

Billing law

A subscription here is a choice, not a trap.

LAW 1

Opt-in, always

You are never enrolled in anything by buying something else. Subscription is a separate, explicit choice at checkout, with the cadence and amount on the same screen — no fine print geometry, no pre-checked boxes.

LAW 2

Next charge, in plain words

Your Manage page always shows: what ships next, the exact date, the exact amount. If you can't state all three in ten seconds, we've failed — tell us and the order's on us.

LAW 3

Cancel in two taps

Pause, skip, or cancel from your Manage page. No chat with support, no retention script, no "are you sure?" maze. Cancelled means cancelled — a cancelled subscription never charges again, and never resurrects.

LAW 4

Paused means paused

A paused subscription does not ship and does not charge. The charge descriptor on your statement says MAANAA — the brand you bought from, not a shell name.

Guarantee law

90 days. Returns on us. No gauntlet.

LAW 5

The full 90 days

Ninety days from delivery to decide — long enough for any product in this line to prove itself, including the slow ones. The date is computed from your delivery, not from whatever an agent tells you.

LAW 6

Return shipping is ours

A prepaid return label comes with every order. A guarantee that costs you money to use is a toll, not a guarantee. Refunds are full — product and shipping, both ways.

LAW 7

No destruction games

Your refund never depends on an authorization number, a photo of a destroyed product, or a settlement offer of 20–30%. Return it, we refund it. If we ever ask for anything else, this page is your receipt.

Freshness & honesty law

The product rules are money rules too.

LAW 8

The 20% floor

Nothing ships with less than 20% of its shelf life remaining. BEST BY and PACKED ON are printed on every pack, so you can audit us from the box in your hand.

LAW 9

Expectations in writing

Every box carries a Timeline Card: what week 1, 4, and 8 typically feel like — including adaptation effects and the sentence "some people feel nothing." If that's you, Law 5 is yours.

LAW 10

Support that answers

Real replies from people who can see your order, your dates, and these laws. If support ever contradicts this page, this page wins — screenshot it.

These laws are printed on the Promise Card inside every box, so the paper in your hand and this page can never quietly diverge. If we ever change a law, the change and its date publish here first.
