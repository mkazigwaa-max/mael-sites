Help, by category

Everything starts at the Virtual Clinical Intake: 28 steps in four blocks — Phenotype & Structural, Fluid & Sebum, Barrier & Sensitivity, Goals + Photo. Your photograph is read on your own device and never uploaded.

The board logic calibrates your custom active protocol from those answers and prescribes your Phase 1 — the 14-Day Attack Stack Box, inside the $118–$148 band. Your reading carries a stamped Board Verification Code.

On Day 15, the re-assessment goes out — what you used, how it's going, and your face. The next batch is reformulated from that gate.

Cycles run 30 days, anchored on the 28th. Days 1–14: the full Attack Stack. Day 15: the iteration gate.

The stack separates one-time hardware — sculpting rollers, gua shas, derma-stamps, LED wands — from the continuous liquids. Hardware drops off after the audit; the liquids continue, fresh each week.

Every piece in the Collection can be browsed and added to the tray on its own; a prescribed stack is claimed as one box at the Prescription Counter.

Shipping is free from $118 — the Phase 1 stack entry price.

Ascension Plus refills auto-ship fresh liquid actives starting Day 14, on the cycle's rhythm.

Held for the house — carrier & timingThe house's carrier and delivery-timing terms are set by the house and published here when they land; nothing about a carrier is promised before then.

Phase 2 is the weekly refill drip: fresh liquid actives every week, with the Weekly Dual Rx Card in the box.

Your subscription auto-charges 3 days before each weekly delivery. The Day-15 re-assessment reformulates every batch, so the drip follows your face, not a fixed formula.

Your drip, your delta and your cycle live in the Member's Room — that is where the membership is managed.

The “Rx-to-Vanity” Formula Guarantee: every serum, cleanser, and barrier glaze is developed under direct dermatological oversight. We strictly adhere to Duke Parameter guidelines for L-Ascorbic Acid stabilization, free-acid glycolic values, and 2:4:2 lipid ratios — delivering prescription-tier results directly to your vanity.

Pieces carry verification codes. A code can be checked on the Authenticity page, against the house registry.

Serums ship with tamper-evident aluminum seals and nitrogen-flushed caps — a seal that arrives broken is a piece to write to the house about, through Contact.

Held for the house — return termsThe board is setting the house's return and refund terms; they are published on the Policies page the moment they are set. Until then, write to the house through Contact before sending anything back.
