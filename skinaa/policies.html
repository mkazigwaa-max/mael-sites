House policies

The cycle

Cycles run 30 days, anchored on the 28th. Days 1–14: the full Attack Stack. Day 15: the iteration gate — a fresh re-assessment reformulates the next batch before it is made.

Prescription & the Phase 1 band

SKINAA is prescribed, not browsed into a cart: the 28-step Virtual Clinical Intake builds your stack, and Phase 1 is claimed as one box inside the $118–$148 band. Individual pieces in the Collection can also be added to the tray on their own. The one-time hardware in a stack — rollers, gua shas, derma-stamps, LED wands — drops off after the audit; the liquids continue.

Shipping

Shipping is free from $118 — the Phase 1 stack entry price. Ascension Plus refills auto-ship fresh liquid actives starting Day 14.

Held for the house — carrier & timingThe house's carrier and delivery-timing terms are set by the house and published in this section when they land.

Ascension Plus billing

Phase 2 is a weekly refill drip. Subscription auto-charges occur 3 days prior to each weekly delivery date. Every batch is reformulated from your Day-15 re-assessment, and each delivery carries the Weekly Dual Rx Card. Membership is managed in the Member's Room.

Privacy

The intake's photograph is read on your own device and never uploaded. Your answers, your consent, and any reading you save stay on your device; the house's pages keep correspondence on this device as well — a static page sends nothing anywhere on its own. Your before & after record is yours: it is kept against your own Day 0 baseline, in the portal, and this house publishes no member's record.

The formula guarantee

The “Rx-to-Vanity” Formula Guarantee: every serum, cleanser, and barrier glaze is developed under direct dermatological oversight. We strictly adhere to Duke Parameter guidelines for L-Ascorbic Acid stabilization, free-acid glycolic values, and 2:4:2 lipid ratios — delivering prescription-tier results directly to your vanity.

Returns & refunds

Held for the house — return termsThe board is setting the house's return and refund terms; they are published in this section the moment they are set. Until then, write to the house through Contact before sending anything back.
