The position

A number you can actually believe

In this corner of the internet a view counter is the scoreboard, and the scoreboard is mostly fiction. There are shops that sell views by the thousand. Everybody knows, and everybody keeps quoting the number anyway.

We count a visitor once a day, we count them on the server where nobody can reach the code, and we publish what we rejected next to what we kept. If your page had a quiet week, it will say so.

  • Nobody sees who visited

    Not us in a dashboard, and not you. Addresses are hashed with a key before they are stored and are never shown to anyone.

  • Rejected views stay visible

    Automated traffic is filtered out and then shown to you, rather than quietly folded into a bigger number.

Questions people ask

Is its.me free?
Yes. Your page, every effect, video and audio backgrounds, the music player, your stats and the file host are free. A few capacity extras, like putting the page on a domain you own, are not part of a free account today.
What does its.me have that other bio sites charge for?
Video and audio backgrounds, the audio visualiser, animated text and background effects, custom fonts and cursors, and full stats. Other bio sites keep those behind a paid plan. Here they come with every account.
Can I show my Discord status on my page?
Yes. Connect Discord and choose what appears: whether you are online, the game you are playing, and what you are listening to on Spotify. Each one has its own switch, and all of them start off.
How are views counted?
A view counts only when a browser confirms it drew your page. Chat previews, bots, prefetches and visitors whose browser asks not to be tracked are not counted, and one network counts as at most five visitors to a page a day. How counting works.
Can I host screenshots on its.me?
Yes. Your account comes with a file host for screenshots and clips, with links on your own name.its.me address. It works with ShareX and with its.me’s Windows app. If the server is ever short of room, new accounts wait a little for it. Set up ShareX.
Do I need an invite code?
No. Anybody can make an account. An invite code still works, and it credits whoever gave it to you.
Is my page tracked or sold?
No. its.me adds no ads, no trackers and no third-party fonts or scripts to your page, and the addresses of the people who visit it are never shown to you or anybody else.
